# Cutoff at rank 90

A night is flagged when its chin rank is 90 or higher: the chin stayed elevated for more of REM than in 90 percent of the healthy nights. Nights with no chin channel are absent. Every remaining night had enough REM to be ranked.

| Group | Nights | Flagged, expert REM | Flagged, detected REM |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Healthy | 15 | 2 | 2 |
| RBD | 22 | 6 | 9 |
| NFLE | 38 | 3 | 1 |
| PLM | 10 | 1 | 1 |
| Insomnia | 9 | 2 | 1 |
| Narcolepsy | 5 | 1 | 1 |
| SDB | 4 | 0 | 2 |
| Bruxism | 2 | 0 | 0 |

On expert REM the cutoff flags 6 of 22 RBD nights and 2 of 15 healthy nights. The other 16 RBD nights stay with the healthy majority. It also flags 3 NFLE nights, 2 insomnia nights, 1 PLM night, and 1 narcolepsy night. Of the 15 flagged nights, 6 are RBD.

On detected REM the cutoff flags 9 of 22 RBD nights and the same 2 healthy nights (`n4`, `n11`). It also flags 2 of 4 SDB nights. Of the 17 flagged nights, 9 are RBD.

The flagged RBD nights on expert REM are `rbd2`, `rbd12`, `rbd13`, `rbd14`, `rbd18`, and `rbd20`. Detected REM adds `rbd4`, `rbd7`, `rbd8`, and `rbd22`, and drops `rbd18`.

This cutoff catches a minority of the RBD nights and also catches nights from other groups. It is a description of this rule on these recordings, not a clinical RBD score and not a Parkinson's score.
