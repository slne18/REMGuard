REMGuard

Upload one EDF with EEG, EOG, and chin EMG. The file is prepared in two steps, in the same way as the nights the model was trained on. First each signal is filtered: EEG keeps 0.3–35 Hz, EOG keeps 0.3–10 Hz, and chin EMG keeps frequencies from 10 Hz upward. Then, when the recording is faster than those bands need, EEG and EOG are resampled to 100 Hz and chin EMG to 256 Hz.

REM is detected from the brain and eye signals. The share of detected REM during which the chin stays above its own threshold is compared with the healthy control nights in the PhysioNet CAP Sleep Database.
